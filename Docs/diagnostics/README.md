# DreamShader diagnostics

Every `DSHnnnn` DreamShader can emit. The leading digit is the stage that raises it:

- **DSH1xxx** --- Driver, source files and imports --- [DSH1xxx.md](DSH1xxx.md)
- **DSH2xxx** --- Lexer and syntax --- [DSH2xxx.md](DSH2xxx.md)
- **DSH3xxx** --- Sections and declarations --- [DSH3xxx.md](DSH3xxx.md)
- **DSH4xxx** --- Graph statements and expressions --- [DSH4xxx.md](DSH4xxx.md)
- **DSH5xxx** --- Builtins -- UE.*, math, Substrate --- [DSH5xxx.md](DSH5xxx.md)
- **DSH6xxx** --- Functions and HLSL codegen --- [DSH6xxx.md](DSH6xxx.md)
- **DSH7xxx** --- Properties, parameters and settings --- [DSH7xxx.md](DSH7xxx.md)
- **DSH8xxx** --- Asset generation and saving --- [DSH8xxx.md](DSH8xxx.md)
- **DSH9xxx** --- Tools, sync and internal invariants --- [DSH9xxx.md](DSH9xxx.md)

Generated by `.skill/gen-diagnostics.ps1`; run it after adding a code.

Messages not yet reachable by a code are still catalogued verbatim in [index.md](index.md),
which stays authoritative until every raise site is tagged.
